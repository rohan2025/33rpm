# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

This policy covers **33rpm** (the macOS app) and **33rpm Bridge** (the Chrome extension).

## What the extension reads

While a YouTube Music tab is open, 33rpm Bridge reads what that tab is playing: the song title, artist, album art
address, whether it is playing or paused, the position in the song, and the Up Next queue.

## Where it goes

Only to the 33rpm app on your own computer, over a local connection to `127.0.0.1`, protected by a pairing token.
The 33rpm app can send back playback commands (play/pause, next, previous, play a chosen song).

Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## What is stored

- The extension saves the pairing token and port you enter, in Chrome's extension storage on your computer.
- The 33rpm app keeps a list of songs you have played (for the crate) and your settings, on your Mac.

You can remove all of it by uninstalling the extension and deleting the app.

## Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and none is used for anything other than showing your music in
33rpm.
